# Frozen physical-phone evaluation

This protocol is prepared before phone inference. It evaluates native offline
repository generation and independently qualified feedback. It cannot establish
prize quality before measurements, and it does not compare two generation algorithms.
The safety and removal scope is in `PHONE_TEST_SCOPE_AND_CLEANUP_20261008.md`.

## Paired feedback comparison and limits

Compare a conventional completed-test exit verdict with a verdict qualified by
task, patch, source, verifier and test identity on the same immutable receipts.
Report valid decision coverage along with unsupported pass/fail claims; rejection
is an unknown result, never an invented failing repair. Preserve separate explicitly
synthetic stale-patch, wrong-task and wrong-source attribution controls. These test
transport boundaries, not the frequency of natural faults or model improvement.
Existing unchanged-assertion import experiments supply the source-selection mechanism
evidence. This comparison alone is not a new repair algorithm or a prize-level finding.

## Measurements and decision

Collect episode elapsed time including shutdown, individual generation latency and
reported tokens, sampled PSS/process/UID memory and unavailable-budget reasons,
start/end temperature/battery, all safety stops and verified phone cleanup. Report
median/range by task and repeat; sampled maximum memory is not an exact peak.
Do not call battery percentage an energy measurement or infer UI latency from
foreground presence. Setup failures, refusals and thermal stops remain in coverage.
Raw journals and device identity remain private; publish only reviewed scalar results.

The paper can claim measured feasibility only after genuine bounded phone episodes
and cleanup receipts, repair utility only after discriminating independent checks,
and feedback benefit only with valid coverage and meaningful real-error evidence.
No leaderboard gain, cross-device generalization, deterministic repeatability,
predictor superiority or first offline phone interface is implied. Assess the final
claim against the five actual paper criteria after results; retain a saved draft and
do not issue a DOI or final entry merely because preparation passes.

## Prepared commands

`python -m scripts.run_phone_evaluation_assignment audit --plan <plan.json>` checks
all frozen inputs/source/APKs without ADB. After connection and read-only device
preflight, use `run` with the exact assignment ID, authorized serial and manually
opened foreground package. One assignment creates its own ownership receipt and
removes both added test packages and their private data at the end. Failed cleanup
requires reconnecting for the dedicated owned cleanup; it is never reported successful
without verification. PC research evidence stays for reproducibility.

`python -m scripts.prepare_phone_evaluation_grading --plan <plan.json> --destination
<new directory>` packages actual outputs only. The independently prepared notebook
`kaggle/75_phone_repeatability_utf8_independent_grading.ipynb` grades that frozen bundle.
No workload begins just because a USB cable is attached.
