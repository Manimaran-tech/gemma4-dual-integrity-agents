Local manuscript addendum, October7 2026. This is not a published Paper Track
revision or a claim of improved repair accuracy. The existing saved manuscript
and supplement remain preserved.

Two further model comparisons use the same six previously exposed FastAPI/Rich
development cases. Exact executed notebook code, frozen archives, original
native interfaces, traces, patches, grading receipts and chronological checkpoint
transports reconcile with directly downloaded output.

Five budget-study assignments remain unattempted. A protected-test patch
conflict halts that study; unknown verifier results are not graded failures.
In the focused study, four v15 verifiers and three v17 verifiers are unknown.
The counts above describe verified successes among attempts, not a complete
known-outcome accuracy comparison.

The correctly loaded v17 prompt produces zero calls to get_status,
run_skill_script or submit_patch in six traces. Both successful v17 production
diffs equal the corresponding parent fixes byte for byte, but add disposable
scripts. The native timeout fallback can capture a successful patch despite no
explicit submit call. Consequently, missing submit calls alone neither establish
patch absence nor explain the official public score.

These results motivate measuring protocol uptake and production diff quality
alongside verification. A bundled structured-skill revision passes two synthetic
native integration controls after an initial working-directory failure; those
controls are not model repairs. Its paired model comparison is newly enqueued
and has no verified outcome at this observation. Neither larger time allowance,
focused instructions nor integration success establishes guard-mediated repair
gain. No unseen-case, causal single-factor or prize-level claim is made.
